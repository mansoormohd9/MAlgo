  WHAT THIS SAYS ABOUT WHETHER TO CONTINUE

    Nifty50 Shariah: the constraint costs 1.65pp a year against Nifty 50,
      wins 47% of months (sign p 0.436) and 43% of rolling 5-year windows.

    Nifty500 Shariah: the constraint pays 0.07pp a year against Nifty 500,
      wins 47% of months (sign p 0.436) and 77% of rolling 5-year windows.

  LIMITS, so no number above is read without them:
    - THE 2022-2026 BLOCK IS THE ONE TO ARGUE WITH. Both pairs
      turn sharply negative there, and the obvious explanation -
      Indian financials led that rally and the screen excludes
      them - is a CONJECTURE. Nothing in this data tests it: an
      index level carries no sector attribution. Treat it as the
      hypothesis to check, not as the reason.
    - An index is not a portfolio: no tax, no brokerage, no
      tracking error, and the administrator rebalances for free.
      The comparison between two of them is clean because both
      carry the same omissions; the LEVEL of either is optimistic.
    - 20 years is one realisation
      containing one crash. The sleeve's +7.94pp became +0.60pp
      on a window it had not seen.
    - Both Shariah indices start at their 2006-12-29 base date,
      so nothing here says anything about 1995-2006.
